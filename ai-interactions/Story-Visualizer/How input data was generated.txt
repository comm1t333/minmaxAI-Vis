The file "Data generation interaction with GEMINI.pdf" shows my interactions with Google Gemini and how the names but more importantly the stories were generated by AI. To shortly summarize: for the names I asked it to generat a gender balanced dictionary of names from different language families. Each language family should contain 20 names. The number 20 turned out to be far too high, and for the first set of predictions (that resulted in big_data.json) I used 8 names and for the 2nd round of inference ( that resulted in small_data.json) I used 4 names. 

For the stories I provided one self written story about a "villain" and I asked GEMINI to generate stories written in similar style about fictional and real characters that I've named. Then I asked the AI to generate 10 stories about heroes of their choosing.

In the end I let Gemini transform those stories in functions, such that I could insert easily names and the grammatically correct pronouns into the story.
